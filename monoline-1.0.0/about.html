WHO WE ARE

Trusted Business Partner for Healthcare, HR & Digital Innovation

Nibhas HRD Solutions Pvt Ltd, established in 2023 in Irinjalakuda, Thrissur, is a leading Doctor Placement and IT solutions company in Kerala, offering end-to-end services that connect technology with talent. We specialize in software development, IT consulting, system integration, digital marketing, and recruitment services- particularly in healthcare staffing and doctor placements across India and abroad.

Our multidisciplinary team works closely with organizations to understand their unique challenges and provide tailored solutions that improve efficiency, strengthen workforce capabilities, and accelerate business growth.
